# D1.1 — Bioenergetic-first knowledge architecture

Version: 1.1.0
Supersedes: D1-knowledge-architecture.md and D1-evidence-policy.md for future Phase D work. The original files remain as historical records.

## Purpose and boundary

The knowledge layer supports interpretation of one participant's longitudinal observations. It is not a diagnostic engine, disease-ranking system, or autonomous treatment recommender. The app remains a local-first tracker. Knowledge updates must never alter participant records.

## Interpretive hierarchy

The project's primary intellectual lens is bioenergetic: Ray Peat-inspired ideas, Gilbert Ling's association-induction hypothesis, and related work by researchers such as Otto Warburg and Albert Szent-Györgyi. This determines which questions and mechanisms the project explores first. It does not make those models established facts.

Every interpretation must keep five layers visible and distinct:

1. **Bioenergetic interpretation — primary lens.** State the model's explanation and identify the author/model it comes from. Label the claim as established, supported, contested, historical, or speculative.
2. **Physiological mechanisms.** Describe relevant established physiology and distinguish demonstrated mechanisms from proposed links.
3. **Mainstream clinical comparison.** Present relevant clinical guidelines, human trials, systematic reviews, and diagnostic standards as a separate comparison. Do not hide this in a footnote.
4. **Critical appraisal.** Show counterevidence, conflicting findings, methodological weaknesses, alternative explanations, and what evidence would change the interpretation.
5. **Safety and escalation.** Identify red flags, contraindications, medication/supplement risks, and when clinician review is appropriate. Safety overrides the preferred interpretive lens.

Do not merge these layers into a single verdict. A user should be able to see where the bioenergetic model agrees with, extends, or conflicts with mainstream clinical evidence.

## Framework labels

Each knowledge record must carry a `framework` label:
- `bioenergetic_primary`
- `physiology`
- `mainstream_comparative`
- `safety`
- `cross_framework`

Each record must also carry a `theoretical_status` label:
- `established_mechanism`
- `supported_model`
- `contested_model`
- `historical_hypothesis`
- `speculative_hypothesis`
- `not_applicable`

Framework priority and scientific certainty are different dimensions. Never infer one from the other.

## Evidence dimensions

Assess claims, not authors or papers as a whole. Preserve these dimensions separately:
- study design and evidence type;
- risk of bias;
- certainty in the specific claim;
- consistency with other relevant findings;
- population match;
- measurement match;
- clinical actionability.

Do not create one numeric evidence score. Mechanistic plausibility is not proof of clinical benefit; association is not causation; a reference interval is not automatically a treatment target; a group average is not an individual's optimal value.

## Assessment output contract

A future assessment engine should return:
- literal observation and time window;
- data quality and missingness;
- relevant context and confounders;
- bioenergetic interpretation;
- established physiological mechanisms;
- mainstream clinical comparison;
- counterevidence and alternative explanations;
- uncertainty and limits of inference;
- safety note and appropriate escalation;
- optional next measurement to improve data quality.

Every interpretation must link to stable knowledge record IDs. No diagnosis, disease probability, medication change, or supplement-dose instruction may be generated from tracker observations alone.

## Bioenergetic source handling

Ray Peat's writings, Ling's association-induction hypothesis, and related historical/theoretical work may be central to the project's exploration. For each claim:
- cite the original source where possible;
- distinguish direct quotations/claims from later interpretations;
- identify whether support comes from human outcomes, human mechanisms, animal work, cell work, or theoretical reasoning;
- include relevant contemporary counterevidence;
- state clearly when human clinical outcome evidence is absent;
- never present a theoretical claim as clinical consensus.

Do not attribute a claim to Peat, Ling, Warburg, or Szent-Györgyi unless the source supports that attribution.

## Safety policy

The mainstream clinical comparison is not merely a disclaimer. It is an independent evidence layer. Safety guidance is mandatory and can override the project's preferred interpretive lens. The tracker must not advise stopping prescribed medication, delaying urgent care, or replacing clinician assessment with a bioenergetic explanation.

## Data and release boundaries

The existing ten dataset IDs and paths are retained for compatibility. Do not rename or delete them as part of D1.1/D2.1. The current app does not consume the knowledge catalog, so these files are architecture and content foundations only; they do not change the live app's behavior.

The project currently targets one participant. Reassess privacy, consent, access control, data handling, and Phase C deployment checks before distributing to additional participants.
