Measurement guidance

  • Use one thermometer and one measurement site consistently.
  • For resting pulse, sit or lie quietly for several minutes before measuring; use the same posture when possible.
  • Record fever, acute illness, medication changes, strenuous exercise and other confounders.
  • Do not deliberately change food, salt, supplements or medication to force a target temperature or pulse.
  • Seek medical assessment for persistent symptoms or concerning changes; urgent symptoms should not wait for trend analysis.
